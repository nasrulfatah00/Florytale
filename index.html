About Us

Hi! We are Florytale, a local idol group from Tegal, Central Java that debuted on November 5, 2023. Our name combines "Flory" and "Tale" to tell the story of blooming flowers. Together with our members, we are here to brighten your day, just like our motto: "Mewarnai hari-harimu seindah bunga bermekaran".

We have just released our debut original single 'Kanvas Musim Semi'! We hope this track brings positive vibes to your day. Listen to it now on your favorite streaming platforms!
